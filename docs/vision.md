## More agency for everyday life

Unison exists to return time and attention to the people who use it. The system
can coordinate administrative work, help people understand complex choices,
support learning and creation, and remain useful through changing needs and
technology.

Success is measured through useful outcomes, understandable decisions,
recoverable actions, and the degree of control each person retains. Personal
context stays available across model and service changes so the relationship can
develop over years.

## One person, one governed assistant

Each person is the principal of their Unison assistant. Identity, consent,
private memory, interaction preferences, credentials, and personal policy are
governed independently. A household can create explicit shared spaces for
mutual plans, devices, schedules, and records while preserving each person's
private spaces.

This person-level foundation supports trust across health, financial, legal,
educational, household, and creative work. Every context space can carry its own
purpose, retention, sharing, and security policy.

## Intelligence expressed in the right form

Unison represents meaning before choosing its expression. The same governed
outcome can become a conversation, a visual composition, a structured Braille
experience, a tactile signal, or a future modality such as sign language or a
brain-computer interface.

Each modality integrates through a common semantic contract. Independent teams
can create native experiences while preserving equivalent meaning, choices,
uncertainty, confirmation, and recovery.

## A system that keeps finding a path

Unison is intent-based computing. Intent captures what a person wants to
accomplish together with their current context, needs, preferences, timing, and
boundaries. It gives the system a stable human objective while models, tools,
devices, and interaction modalities remain flexible.

Reliable skills, tools, and workflows provide consistent routes for familiar
work. Governed inference helps resolve novel requests and ambiguous situations.
When recurring requests reveal a useful pattern, Unison can prepare a proposed
capability for human review. Accepted proposals become versioned, testable, and
inspectable building blocks.

This creates a growing local repertoire while preserving natural interaction.
The person can begin with intent and let the system assemble an appropriate
route from the capabilities available at that moment.

## From software platform to household utility

The current project is experimental software for capable Ubuntu systems. The
longer-term vision is a professionally deployable personal intelligence
appliance with modular compute, memory, storage, networking, radios, sensing,
security, power, and cooling.

The hardware path includes estimated bills of materials, qualification data,
open custom schematics, repair guidance, and a distinct enclosure. Incremental
hardware profiles can expand local capability before the integrated appliance
is ready.

Unison names the complete assistant platform and experience. UnisonOS names the
Linux appliance runtime and operating surface. The personal node is the trusted
household system where identity, context, policy, and continuity live.

## Open source as a product property

Open development makes trust inspectable and participation practical. Public
contracts define authority. Evidence labels distinguish unit tests, simulation,
hosted CI, physical hardware, and participatory evaluation. Replaceable modules
let the project adopt new models, storage systems, modalities, and hardware
while a person's identity and accumulated context remain continuous.
